        Where the numbers come from

        • Standby watts are the median of measured units per product from Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory's Products That Use Standby Power table (Nov 2022). Each device shows its measured range and how many units were tested.
        • Yearly kWh = watts × 8,760 hours ÷ 1,000. This assumes the device sits idle all year, so it's an upper bound for things you actually use a lot.
        • Price is your state's average residential price for July 2026 from the EIA Electric Power Monthly, Table 5.6.A. Change it to match your bill.
        • CO₂ uses your state's output emission rate from EPA eGRID 2023, Table 3 (US average: 767 lb per MWh, or 0.348 kg per kWh).
        • Miles and cars use the EPA's figures of about 400 g CO₂ per mile and 4.6 metric tons per year for a typical passenger vehicle.
        • Households: about 133 million in the US in 2024, from the Census Bureau's Families and Living Arrangements release (38.5 million one-person households is 29% of all households).
        • Power strip payback assumes a $25 switched or smart strip. That's an assumption, not a measurement.

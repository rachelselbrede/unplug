    Where the numbers come from

    • Standby watts are the median of measured units per product from Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory's Products That Use Standby Power table (Nov 2022). Each device shows its measured range and how many units were tested.
    • Yearly kWh = watts × 8,760 hours ÷ 1,000. This assumes the device sits idle all year, so it's an upper bound for things you actually use a lot.
    • Cost uses the US average residential price of 18.31 ¢/kWh for July 2026 from the EIA Electric Power Monthly, Table 5.6.A. Change it to match your bill.
    • CO₂ uses the US average grid rate of 767 lb per MWh (0.348 kg per kWh) from EPA eGRID 2023. Your grid may be cleaner or dirtier.
    • Miles use the EPA's figure of about 400 g CO₂ per mile for a typical passenger vehicle.
